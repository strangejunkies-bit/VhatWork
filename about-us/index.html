About Fresh Work Factory

A Production Partner
for the Whole Brand.

Fresh Work Factory helps businesses take a brand from concept to the places customers actually see it—print, signage, packaging, apparel, web and digital channels.

Built for Businesses That Need More Than One Vendor.

Fresh Work Factory is based in Ann Arbor, Michigan and serves businesses across Southeast Michigan, Metro Detroit and projects that extend beyond the local market. The work spans graphic design, commercial printing, signage, specialty packaging, apparel and digital execution.

The advantage is continuity. A business card, storefront sign, cup, uniform and website should not look like five separate companies designed them. We help keep the visual language connected while still respecting the production requirements of each medium.

Who we work with

Restaurants, cafes, coffee shops, retailers, contractors, real estate professionals, healthcare offices, schools, nonprofits, event teams, cannabis businesses, startups and established companies all have different production needs. The common requirement is clear: the work has to look professional when it reaches the customer.

Local presence, broader reach

Ann Arbor and the surrounding Southeast Michigan market are home base. Print, packaging, apparel and digital projects can also be coordinated for clients across Michigan, the Midwest and nationwide depending on the job.

What we bring together

The service mix spans brand identity, business printing, marketing collateral, stickers and labels, direct mail, apparel, packaging, large-format print, yard signs, vehicle graphics, storefront and interior signage, promotional products, web design and digital marketing. Clients can start with one need and expand the system without rebuilding the brand from scratch each time.
