Signage Questions

Details to settle before fabrication.

A few practical answers before the project is quoted and produced.

What neon and LED signs options are available?

The options listed above cover the most common neon and LED signs requests. Final recommendations depend on size, viewing distance, material, mounting, illumination and the installation environment.

Can you help with artwork and sizing?

Yes. We can prepare or adapt artwork around final dimensions, viewing distance, fabrication method and installation requirements so the sign is designed for the space where it will be used.

What affects signage turnaround?

Timing depends on size, materials, fabrication method, illumination, proof approval and installation requirements. Any permit or site-specific timing is confirmed separately when applicable.
