Choose stock, coating, texture and specialty finishing around the use case rather than defaulting to the most expensive option.

Available options

Use the list below as a starting point. If you need a variation, specialty size, material or finish that is not shown, include it in the quote request.

Standard

  • 14pt Profit Maximizer
  • Matte Finish (14pt/16pt)
  • UV High Gloss (14pt/16pt)
  • AQ Semi Gloss (14pt/16pt)

Premium

  • Laminated (Gloss/Silk)
  • Writable Cards

Specialty

  • Metallic Foil
  • Kraft
  • Durable/Waterproof

  • Pearl
  • Die Cut
  • Soft Touch
  • Painted Edge (32pt)
  • Ultra Smooth
  • Folded Business Cards

Print Questions

Production details that matter.

A few practical answers before the project is quoted and produced.

What business card options are available?

The options listed above cover the most common business card requests. If you need a different size, stock, finish or quantity, include it in the quote and we will confirm the best production route.

Can you help prepare artwork for business cards?

Yes. We can review supplied files or prepare artwork so size, bleed, color, resolution and finishing requirements are correct before production.

What affects turnaround time?

Timing depends on quantity, stock or material availability, finishing, proof approval and the current production schedule. Your quote will include the expected turnaround for the specific job.
