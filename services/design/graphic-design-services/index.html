Design Questions

Build the files for real production.

A few practical answers before the project is quoted and produced.

What graphic design services are available?

Fresh Work Factory supports identity, flyers, postcards, brochures, business cards, banners, posters, product graphics, apparel artwork and other production-ready design needs listed above.

Can you prepare files for print and production?

Yes. Design work can be built around final dimensions, bleed, color, readability and output requirements so the files are ready for the intended print, signage, packaging or apparel application.

How is design turnaround determined?

Timing depends on scope, number of concepts or pieces, supplied content, revision rounds and the final production requirements. Expected timing is confirmed with the project quote.
