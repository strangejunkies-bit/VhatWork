Questions Before Production

What to Know About Coffee Shop Packaging

Final specifications depend on the job. These answers cover the most common planning questions before a quote is built.

What coffee shop packaging options do you offer?

Fresh Work Factory supports options including Ceramic Cups, Clear Cup Lids, Clear PET Cups, plus additional formats listed on this page. If your specification is not listed, send the size, quantity, material or use case with the quote request.

Can you help with artwork for coffee shop packaging?

Yes. Design support can be coordinated with coffee shop packaging production so dimensions, readability and output requirements are considered before the job is produced.

How is turnaround determined?

Turnaround depends on the specific coffee shop packaging format, quantity, finishing, material availability and proof approval. A project quote should confirm timing before production begins.
