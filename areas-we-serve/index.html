Service Area

Local Enough to Meet.
Built to Ship.

Fresh Work Factory is based in Ann Arbor and serves Metro Detroit, Southeast Michigan and production projects that can be coordinated nationwide.

Ann Arbor Is Home Base.

Local projects benefit from a production partner that understands the businesses, campuses, event cycles and growth around Washtenaw County and Metro Detroit. Fresh Work Factory supports companies and organizations across the region while also coordinating shippable print, packaging, apparel and digital projects outside Michigan.

Southeast Michigan & Michigan markets

Ann Arbor, Ypsilanti, Saline, Dexter, Canton, Plymouth, Novi, Farmington Hills, Detroit, Dearborn, Livonia, Southfield, Royal Oak, Ferndale, Warren, Sterling Heights, Lansing, Flint, Grand Rapids, Kalamazoo and surrounding communities.

Midwest and nationwide projects

Many printed, packaged, apparel and digital deliverables can be coordinated beyond the immediate region. Final shipping, installation or service coverage depends on the specific project and should be confirmed during quoting.

Planning a project outside Michigan

For work that can be shipped or coordinated remotely, include the destination, quantity and deadline with the project details. Installation-dependent signage and specialty work should be confirmed case by case before production.

Need an installation outside Ann Arbor?
Include the project location in the quote request so installation, freight or coordination requirements can be evaluated before pricing.
