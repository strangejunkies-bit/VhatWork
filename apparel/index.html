Apparel
Decoration That Holds Up.

Choose the decoration method around the artwork, garment, quantity and end use. We support screen printing, DTG, sublimation and embroidery for teams, businesses, events and branded merchandise.

Service Directory

Custom Apparel Without the Guesswork

Screen printing, direct-to-garment, sublimation and embroidery for custom shirts, uniforms, hats, jackets and branded apparel.

01

Screen Printing

Screen printing remains a strong choice for durable graphics and repeat quantities. We help match garment, ink coverage and artwork setup to the intended use.

→02

DTG Printing

DTG is useful when artwork contains many colors or smaller quantities are needed. We help determine whether the garment and design are a good fit for direct-to-garment production.

→03

Sublimation

Sublimation embeds artwork into compatible fabric, making it useful for all-over designs and performance garments. We plan artwork around panels, seams and final garment construction.

→04

Embroidery

Embroidery adds a durable, dimensional finish to polos, hats, jackets, bags and patches. We prepare artwork with stitch behavior and garment placement in mind.
